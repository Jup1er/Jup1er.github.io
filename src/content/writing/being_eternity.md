Is a fleshly embrace merely a manifestation of souls intertwining? Perhaps it is simply the absolute limit of what our physical bodies can do to peer into the soul.

I look at the sculpture of Cupid and Psyche locked in an embrace. Their embrace lasts only a fleeting moment, yet it has lingered in stone for millennia.

What the stone records is not an action, but the evidence of truth.

Yet, under the dimension of time, even that is not eternity.

We yearn to find eternity within time, yet we are destined never to grasp it.

What is it that we wish to catch? Is it the evidence to prove that we once existed, to prove that what we felt was real?  
But what does it mean to remain? And how must we remain for it to be counted as proof?  
Is not what we feel in the present moment the most powerful evidence of all?

We piece together the truth of the world by overlaying countless similar feelings, experienced individually by countless versions of "myself" in similar circumstances.

If the feelings of countless "selves" toward similar events can be taken as truth, then why should the feelings of a single "me" toward a unique event that happens solely to myself be any less real?

The events in our memory, and the souls shaped after experiencing and feeling them—have they not already broken free from the coordinate axes of time?

They already exist, so what is it that you are still trying to prove, and by what means? Is it not like copying the words of ancient saints and sages over and over again just to prove they once existed? But does the existence of a saint ever need to be proven this way? Can it even be proven this way?

Existence already exists, and no one can erase it. Therefore, existence is eternity.

We are merely experiencing eternity in its myriad forms.

Our souls, our consciousness, traverse layers of form to feel countless unnamed eternities.

Yet these feelings are so easily simplified, misunderstood, and forgotten.

That is why we keep creating. We write poems, write letters, write stories. We are leaving traces.

Our souls, at this very moment, are growing in a world that demands the validation of the body.

And so, lies came into this world.

Precisely because truth is so difficult to verify, people in this world fabricate illusions out of all kinds of motives.

So, just ask your own feelings. Feelings never lie—it is just that their voice is too faint, and you must listen with the utmost care.
